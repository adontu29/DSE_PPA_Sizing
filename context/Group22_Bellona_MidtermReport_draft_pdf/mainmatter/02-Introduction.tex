\chapter{Project Objectives}

The BELLONA project develops an unmanned aerial system for the safe interception, control, and recovery of unauthorized high-altitude balloons. The design objective is to remove the balloon-payload system from endangered airspace while avoiding intentional fragmentation, uncontrolled payload release, and unnecessary risk to people, infrastructure, and recovery crews.

The midterm design objective is to define and justify a feasible conceptual configuration. This includes establishing the mission requirements, identifying the main design drivers, comparing candidate concepts through a traceable trade-off, and developing the selected concept far enough to assess performance, interfaces, risks, verification needs, sustainability, and financial viability.

The selected system must detect or receive target cues, approach the balloon-payload system, capture or secure the payload region, and guide the combined system to a designated recovery area. The design is constrained by mission time, operating altitude, wind and gust conditions, payload mass, recovery accuracy, regulatory safety, environmental impact, and cost.
